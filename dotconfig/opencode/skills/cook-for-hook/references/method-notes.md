## Worked adaptation: consumption shapes thinking

This example is original to the skill, not a quotation or example from the video.

**Brief:** an X account sharing selected ideas; invite readers to choose their influences deliberately.

**Isolate:** What you consume shapes what you think.

**Commit:** Keep that causal relationship at the center, rather than adding separate promises about productivity, wealth, or success.

**Deep-fry directions:**

- Reverse the apparent agency: you choose what to read; it also shapes you.
- Identity contrast: apparently independent thinking contains other people's ideas.
- Rename the phenomenon: choosing a reading list is choosing future thoughts.
- Raise the stakes: passive consumption can make influence less deliberate.

**Rough extreme:** “Your reading list owns your brain.”

This has tension but turns influence into total control. Walk it back to preserve agency and avoid an unsupported absolute.

**Polished hook:** “Your reading list is rehearsing your next thought.”

**Landing:** “What you encounter repeatedly becomes easier to recall. Choose the ideas you want close at hand.”

**Naming adaptation:** `YourNextThought` (15 characters). It compresses the idea into the reader's next encounter with the account. Availability is unknown.

The example demonstrates a process, not a mandatory template or a claim that this wording will outperform alternatives.
