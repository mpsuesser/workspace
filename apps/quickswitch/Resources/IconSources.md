# Bundled transparent marks

These are app-identification marks, not QuickSwitch branding. Their respective
owners retain their trademarks. Assets are normalized onto transparent 512px
canvases, without a dock tile or rounded-square mask.

- Ghostty: `public/ghostty-logo.svg` in
  <https://github.com/ghostty-org/website>, rasterized without changing the mark.
- T3 Code: standalone navigation wordmark from <https://t3.codes>, rendered dark
  charcoal (`#202a27`) for contrast on light wallpapers.
- Curation Space Admin: transparent violet-and-gold
  `apps/admin-dashboard/public/icons/admin-512.png` from the owner's Curation
  Space checkout, retaining the Admin app's original colors.
- Linear: `icons/linear.svg` from
  <https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons>, rendered deep violet (`#5145a6`)
  for contrast on light wallpapers.
- Chrome: full-color `src/chrome/chrome.svg` from
  <https://github.com/alrra/browser-logos>, retaining its original colors.
- Rex: the `Rexie` image resource in the installed Rex Beta app bundle. This is the
  standalone mascot, not a cutout of its dock icon.
